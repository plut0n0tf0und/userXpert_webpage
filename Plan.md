# UserXpert Landing Page

## Goal

Build a premium, modern, high-converting landing page for **UserXpert**, a digital solutions agency.

This is NOT a portfolio website.

This is NOT a corporate website.

This is a conversion-focused landing page whose primary objective is to make business owners contact us via WhatsApp or request a free consultation.

The design should feel trustworthy, premium, clean, and modern without relying on trendy effects that hurt usability.

---

# Brand Personality

UserXpert helps businesses grow using digital solutions.

We don't just build websites.

We solve business problems through technology.

Our services include:

- Custom Websites
- Website Redesign
- Business Automation
- AI Chatbots
- E-commerce Solutions
- Analytics Dashboards
- Portfolio Websites
- Landing Pages
- Custom Internal Business Tools

---

# Design Direction

Take inspiration from:

- Linear
- Stripe
- Framer
- Vercel

BUT...

Do NOT copy them.

Avoid startup clichés.

Avoid excessive gradients.

Avoid glassmorphism.

Avoid huge empty spaces.

Avoid unnecessary animations.

Avoid distracting backgrounds.

The website should look premium even after 5 years.

Think timeless.
